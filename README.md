## 🧭 Overview

FaceFilter 2026 is a desktop-grade portrait transformation environment for Windows platforms. It blends classical retouching principles — proportion, symmetry, tone, and texture — with modern computational pipelines so that enthusiasts, content creators, and professional retouchers can shape a face with intent rather than guesswork.

The project pages here exist to guide you through obtaining the application, configuring it for your machine, and mastering the feature set. Whether you are smoothing a single headshot or building a batch of studio-ready portraits, the goal is the same: fewer clicks, more control, and results that look like they were crafted by hand because, in effect, they were.

## 💡 Why FaceFilter 2026 Exists

Most editors treat faces as just another region of pixels. FaceFilter 2026 treats them as architecture. Cheekbones have load-bearing structure. Jawlines have perspective. Eyes have focal hierarchy. Once you start editing with that mental model, the tool stops feeling like software and starts feeling like collaboration.

The 2026 edition was assembled around three convictions:

1. **Precision beats presets.** Presets are a starting point, never the destination.
2. **Speed should never cost subtlety.** Fast tools should still produce believable results.
3. **The interface should disappear.** When the UI works, you forget it's there.

## 🌀 Workflow Walkthrough

1. **Import or Capture** — Drag in a portrait or connect a compatible camera for tethered capture.
2. **Subject Detection** — The app outlines detected faces and ranks them by prominence.
3. **Proportion Pass** — Adjust cheek width, jaw taper, forehead height, and eye spacing.
4. **Skin Pass** — Even tone, reduce transient blemishes, preserve pore structure.
5. **Detail Pass** — Sharpen lashes, brighten irises, refine lip contour.
6. **Color Pass** — Warmth, contrast, and film emulation applied to the whole portrait.
7. **Export** — Choose output size, format, and embed profile data for downstream apps.

Each pass is reversible, stackable, and saved to the project file.

## ⚡ Performance Notes

FaceFilter 2026 leverages hardware acceleration wherever possible. On supported GPUs, previews render at full resolution without lag. If you notice sluggishness, try:

- Reducing preview resolution to half-size during edits, then export at full quality.
- Closing background applications that compete for VRAM.
- Updating graphics drivers to the latest manufacturer release.
- Enabling "Fast Preview" mode for large batch sessions.

Memory usage scales primarily with image resolution and layer count. For convenience, a "budget mode" caps layer history to keep RAM usage predictable.

## 🧬 Design Philosophy

Every panel in FaceFilter 2026 was designed with a single question: does this make the artist's job easier? If the answer was no, the panel was redesigned or removed. Tools are grouped by intent rather than by algorithm, and keyboard shortcuts mirror those found in popular creative software so muscle memory transfers cleanly.

We believe in quiet interfaces. Not empty ones — quiet ones. Everything you need sits close at hand, and nothing you don't need intrudes on the canvas.
